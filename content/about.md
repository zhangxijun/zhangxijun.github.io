iBetterLife is an independent review site for US shoppers. We focus on health, wellness,
and everyday products sold on **Amazon** and **iHerb**, and we publish plain-English
buying guides instead of marketing copy.

## What we do

- **Hands-on reviews** — we buy (or have long-term experience with) the products we recommend.
- **Side-by-side comparisons** — clear tables so you can compare price, ingredients, and value.
- **Deal picks** — products that are genuinely worth it at their current price.

## How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small
commission at **no extra cost to you**. Read our full
[affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
