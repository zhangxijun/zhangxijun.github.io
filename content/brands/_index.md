Brand selection matters more than most labels suggest. The same 500 mg of turmeric costs
three times more under one label than another, and house brands can undercut the name
brands without giving up third-party testing. This list covers the brands a US shopper
actually runs into: drugstore staples, gummy mass market, sports nutrition, and the
practitioner lines sold through clinics. Each entry links to the
brand's page on Amazon and iHerb. It grows as we cover more categories.
