California Gold Nutrition launched in 2013 as iHerb's in-house supplement line. It now carries more SKUs than any other brand on iHerb and consistently tops the best-seller lists for fish oil, CoQ10, probiotics and basic vitamins. The brand does not operate retail channels outside of iHerb and Amazon; its low price point is possible because it skips pharmacy distribution and marketing overhead.

What sets CGN apart from other budget lines is that it is not a rebranded commodity formula. The brand uses patented raw materials where it matters: Albion TRAACS for minerals, OptiMSM for joint products, kd-pür for fish oil and Astalif for astaxanthin. The "Gold" in the name comes from USP-grade vitamin C; that single product now has 384,000+ iHerb ratings.

## 1. What the brand actually is

CGN's identity rests on three things.

**iHerb exclusive distribution.** Almost every product ships directly from iHerb warehouses, which is why the prices undercut independent brands by 20–40%. The trade-off is visibility: you will not see CGN bottles in Walgreens or Target.

**Patented raw materials.** Where a budget brand would pick generic magnesium oxide, CGN uses Albion TRAACS bisglycinate. Where a competitor uses non-branded MSM, CGN specifies OptiMSM. The company does not patent-formulate its own end products, but it consistently selects better starting ingredients than most budget lines.

**Third-party testing via iHerb's iTested program.** Selected SKUs (CoQ10, D3, some probiotics) carry the iTested badge, which means iHerb bought bottles anonymously and tested them for potency and purity. Coverage is SKU-specific, not brand-wide.

## 4. Awards and certifications

CGN products carry certifications from several third parties:

- **iTested Verified** (iHerb's anonymous testing program): CoQ10 100 mg, D3 5,000 IU, LactoBif 30, Gold C
- **Non-GMO, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free**: Company-wide formula declarations
- **USP grade raw materials**: CoQ10, vitamin C, creatine (raw material certification, not end-product USP Verified)
- **OptiMSM ingredient certification**: Joint care formulas
- **Albion TRAACS ingredient certification**: Magnesium chelate
- **kd-pür fish oil ingredient certification**: Omega-3 lines

There are no independently issued product awards (Vity Awards, Better Nutrition, etc.) in the brand's history. iHerb's "#1 Best Seller" badges are sales-rank positions, not awards.

## 5. Where the brand is weaker

CGN has three real limitations. First, Amazon availability is inconsistent; Amazon listings are created by third-party sellers, not by iHerb, and prices on Amazon are usually higher than iHerb. Second, not every product is iTested; the program covers SKUs selectively, so you need to check the badge on individual bottles rather than trusting the entire line. Third, some formulas trade form for price: the magnesium bisglycinate uses 200 mg elemental per two capsules (30 cents) while NOW's magnesium glycinate delivers 200 mg per two capsules at about the same price, and Garden of Life's formulas use organic and non-GMO certification that CGN does not seek.

## 6. Buying notes

Almost every CGN product is cheaper on iHerb than on Amazon, so shop iHerb first. The iTested program adds confidence but coverage is partial; if testing matters to you, check the badge before buying. Prices swing during iHerb's 20–30% coupon events, which happen every 2–3 weeks. The Omega-3 premium fish oil 100-softgel bottle is the most consistent price leader on the site.

CGN products ship worldwide from iHerb warehouses, so international shoppers will save even more relative to local supplement brands. If you need Amazon, use the formula-specific search links rather than the iHerb product page — Amazon third-party sellers may list different sizes or older formulations.
