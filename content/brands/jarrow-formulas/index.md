Jarrow Formulas started in 1977 in Los Angeles as a mail-order supplement brand that specialized in niche ingredients other brands ignored. Today it carries roughly 200 SKUs across probiotics, methylation, brain health and specialty minerals — categories where "what you sell" is less important than "what branded ingredient or clinical study sits on the bottle." Jarrow is not a budget brand: MagMind at 93 cents per serving and Meriva curcumin at 76 cents are among the most expensive single-ingredient supplements we have reviewed. The premium is rooted in Jarrow's strategy: every flagship product is built around a branded or patented ingredient with published clinical data, not commodity extracts.

In January 2022, Jarrow merged with Natrol under a parent company called Vytalogy Wellness, backed by New Mountain Capital private equity. The product lines have remained separate, but Natrol's larger manufacturing and distribution capacity now supports Jarrow's SKU expansion.

## 1. What the brand actually is

Jarrow Formulas' identity rests on three things.

**Branded ingredients over commodity extracts.** Where Doctor's Best uses generic Curcumin C3 + BioPerine, Jarrow uses Meriva (phospholipid curcumin phytosome), which has dramatically better bioavailability. Where NOW uses generic glycinate magnesium, Jarrow uses Magtein (magnesium L-threonate), the only form with evidence of crossing the blood-brain barrier. Where most brands list "probiotic blend," Jarrow uses specific named strains with clinical data (Jarro-Dophilus EPS, Fem-Dophilus GR-1/RC-14, 299v, Saccharomyces boulardii).

**Methylation and MTHFR focus.** Jarrow was one of the first supplement brands to market directly to people with MTHFR mutations and methylation issues. Its methyl folate (Quatrefolic), methyl B12 and fully active B complex products all use bioactive cofactors rather than synthetic precursors that require MTHFR conversion.

**Single-purpose clinical formulas.** Jarrow prefers narrow, focused products — Ideal Bowel Support for IBS, Fem-Dophilus for women's urogenital health, MagMind for cognition — over broad-spectrum blends. If Jarrow sells one thing for one specific condition, it has a clinical study to justify it.

## 5. Where the brand is weaker

Three limitations. First, premium pricing: Magtein at 93 cents per serving and Meriva at 76 cents are significantly more expensive than commodity alternatives, and only justified if you specifically need those branded ingredients. Second, limited catalog at the mainstream supplement level (no children's, no protein, no pet supplements). Third, no USP or NSF end-product verification — the branded ingredients carry their own certifications (Quatrefolic, Magtein, Meriva, Kaneka), but Jarrow does not independently verify final product potency at the manufacturing level.

## 6. Buying notes

Amazon and iHerb prices are consistent across most Jarrow products. The Methyl Folate and MagMind products on Amazon have better buy-box pricing than iHerb for some periods, so cross-check before buying. For probiotics, always check CFU count *before* expiration — probiotic potency declines with time, and older bottles may have significantly less CFU than stated on the label. The dedicated clinical probiotics (299v, GR-1/RC-14, S. boulardii) are the products that justify the Jarrow premium; generic 10-strain probiotics from other brands at lower CFU and lower prices are better value for general gut health.
